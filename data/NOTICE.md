# Data notice

`data/raw_docs/` holds unmodified Markdown / reStructuredText documentation files copied from the official
repositories of FastAPI, pandas, scikit-learn and Pydantic at the release tags listed in the paper (Table 1).
These files remain under their upstream licences and copyright (see each repository's `LICENSE`); they are
redistributed here only so the study can be reproduced offline. `sections.jsonl` and `pairs.json` are derived
from them by `scripts/build_sections.py`.
